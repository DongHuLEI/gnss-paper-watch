用观测回答「波前能走多远」

We have analyzed multiple MSTID events using total electron content (TEC) measurements from a dense network of dual- frequency GNSS receivers across India, GNSS radio occultation (RO) profiles, and ionosonde observations from New Delhi during May-July 2025, a high solar activity period.

同一季节里，波前停止位置并不固定

作者汇总 21 次事件，波前南界约在 17°N 至 25°N，所见波前呈西北—东南走向且向西南传播。背景 VTEC 的局地比较没有检出显著关联，映射后的 Es 南界在有可用 RO 的子样本中则与波前南界同向变化；这不是对磁力线电场的直接测量。

However, the southernmost latitude reached by the MSTID wavefronts varied across these events from ~17°N to ~25°N geographic latitude.

02

Research gap

作者报告

仅靠 F 区不稳定性，解释不了什么

Perkins instability 能解释典型波前方向和极化电场，但作者指出其单独线性增长偏慢。风切变产生的薄 Es 层可带有极化电场，沿地磁场映射后给 F 区扰动提供正反馈。生成阶段存在耦合，不自动回答传播阶段波前会在哪里衰减。

对照原文证据
Although, Perkins instability explains the preferential alignment and associated polarization electric fields of nighttime MSTIDs, its inherently low linear growth rate (~10-4 s-1) remains a major limitation in explaining the development of MSTIDs (Kelley & Makela, 2001; Narayanan et al., 2018; Otsuka et al., 2007). Theoretical and observational studies have demonstrated that E-F region coupling significantly enhances this low linear growth rate of Perkins instability by providing positive feedback
原文 · PDF 第 4 页 ↗
These polarization electric fields can map along geomagnetic field lines into the F-region, thereby reinforcing the Perkins instability and promoting the growth of MSTDs (Narayanan et al., 2018; Otsuka et al., 2007; Patgiri et al., 2024c).

作者归纳的既有解释包括赤道电离异常（EIA）峰附近的阻力、低背景密度，以及午夜亮度波等条件。本文的增量是对照波前南界与磁映射 Es 南界，并与同期背景 VTEC 作比较；不是声称首次发现 Es 与 MSTID 共同发生，也不是一次全面查新。

Shiokawa et al. (2002) suggested that the usually higher ion drag in the equatorial ionization

算法主线

作者报告

先用 DVTEC 定波前，再用 VTEC 描背景

双频 GNSS 相位与伪距按 30 秒采样，按 350 km 薄壳求斜向与垂直 TEC，截去低于 35° 的观测。VTEC 用于背景对照；减去 1 小时居中滑均值得 DVTEC，按 0.25° × 0.25° 格点成图，再作时域聚合及空间移动均值，跟踪交替波带而不是读绝对电子含量的峰。

对照原文证据
Dual-frequency carrier phase and pseudo-range measurements were sampled at a 30-second interval, which were processed to extract Slant and Vertical Total Electron Content (STEC and VTEC) following the methodology of Seemala (2023), assuming an Ionospheric Pierce Point (IPP) altitude of 350 km. An elevation cutoff of 35° was applied to mitigate errors associated with the thin-shell approximation.

然后把探测位置映到同一高度

多星座掩星的 GPS L1 信噪比先按 20 km 背景窗归一化，再计算 1 km 滑动标准差作为幅度闪烁指标；用限定高度内的阈值识别薄层。AACGM-v2 把 E 区切点沿地磁场映到 350 km 穿刺点高度。只有这个足迹才和 F 区波前比较，不能把切点原纬度当足迹。

提升与证据

作者报告

两晚对照先给定位，再看总体

20 May 2025 的波前约到 17°N，沿途观测到 Es；27 May 2025 则局限于约 25°N，北段仍观测到 Es，但预期南进路径和新德里电离图未见同样响应。两晚不是简单的「有 Es」与「无 Es」：变化的是覆盖到哪里，以及哪里有记录。

对照原文证据
On 20 May 2025, the MSTID wavefronts propagated into the low latitudes, reaching as far south as ~17°N geographic latitude, extending across nearly the entire Indian mainland (Figures 1h-1j). However, on 27 May 2025, the MSTID remained confined to ~25°N geographic latitude (Figures 1k-1m).

On 20 May 2025, Es-layers were observed over the northern latitudes (Figures 3i-3j) as well as along the equatorward propagation path of the MSTID (Figures 3a-3h). The New Delhi ionograms also showed a blanketing type Es-layer near 100 km during this event
原文 · PDF 第 10 页 ↗
On 27 May 2025, MSTID wavefronts were confined to the northern region (>25°N), where Es activity was also observed (Figures 4c-4d, 4g- 4h). However, unlike 20 May 2025, no Es activity was detected along the expected equatorward propagation path of the MSTIDs (Figures 4a-4b, 4e-4f, 4i-4l). In addition, New Delhi ionosonde also didn’t detect any Es-layer (Figures 4m-4p).

背景散点没有给出清楚的线性关联

最南纬度与波前周围的背景 VTEC 作配对，正文报告 r 约 0.1、p 约 0.66。这里读到的是选定事件和背景窗口下未检出关联，不是证明背景等离子体一定无影响。EIA 北峰衰减以后也有波停在偏北位置；这削弱「只用背景解释停止位置」的说法。

However, our observations show that after the nighttime decay of the EIA crest, several MSTIDs remained confined to higher latitudes despite low background VTEC, while other events propagated into the lower latitudes under comparable or even higher background VTEC conditions. Furthermore, the VTEC keograms for all 21 events and the comparison between background VTEC and the lowest MSTID latitude show no correlation (Figure 2). 磁映射之后的最南 Es 足迹才是关联量

在 21 次事件中，17 次于波前最南时刻有可用 RO；作者对照该时刻最南已检出 Es 的 350 km 足迹，正文报告 r 约 0.6、p 约 0.01。正相关在这里意味着两个地理纬度一起偏南或偏北，并非 Es 越往北，MSTID 南侵越深。

对照原文证据
For 17 out of the 21 events, the RO observations were available when the MSTIDs reached their southernmost extent. The latitude of this extent was positively correlated (r ~0.6; p ~0.01) with the latitude of the southernmost observed Es-layer’s footprint at 350 km (Figure 5c). However, the apparent not-so-strong correlation is likely due to the simultaneous RO profiles being unavailable (in some cases) close to the southernmost extent of MSTID fronts. The overall trend nevertheless suggests that MSTIDs extend to lower latitudes when active Es-layers are also observed at lower latitudes.

外推之前，先确认比较基准和共同分母

对照原始英文摘要
This study provides the first observational evidence that E-F region electrodynamic coupling regulates the southernmost latitudinal extent of nighttime mid-latitude medium-scale traveling ionospheric disturbances (MSTIDs) over the Indian subcontinent. We have analyzed multiple MSTID events using total electron content (TEC) measurements from a dense network of dual-frequency GNSS receivers across India, GNSS radio occultation (RO) profiles, and ionosonde observations from New Delhi during May-July 2025, a high solar activity period. The lowest geographic latitude, at which MSTIDs wavefronts reached, varied between 17°N and 25°N. Their low-latitude extension showed no significant correlation with background TEC, but was positively correlated with the geomagnetically mapped southernmost latitude of sporadic-E (Es) layers identified from GNSS-RO measurements. These events occurred during high solar activity, when F-region MSTID growth is generally suppressed. Therefore, our results suggest that electrodynamic coupling with the underlying Es-layers is essential for sustaining MSTIDs and enabling their low-latitude penetration during solar maxima.
