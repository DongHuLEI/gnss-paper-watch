Research gap

作者报告

GNSS 的 MEO 链路弱点

作者把研究缺口的起点放在 GNSS 的 MEO 链路：传播距离长、到达终端信号弱，遇到遮挡、干扰、多径与欺骗时更脆弱，城市峡谷和室内尤其明显。这使传统 GNSS 在这些场景中的可用性与连续性受限。

对照原文证据
However, GNSS satellites are primarily deployed in medium Earth orbit (MEO), where the long propagation distance results in relatively weak received signals at the user terminal, reducing their robustness to blockage, interference, multipath, and spoofing.
原文 · S1.p1.1 ↗
These limitations become particularly evident in urban canyons, indoor environments, and other GNSS-challenged scenarios.
原文 · S1.p1.1 ↗
作者报告

LEO 的链路、带宽与几何机会

LEO 的较低轨道、较宽带宽和更大星座分别对应更强的接收信号、更细的时延分辨与更好的几何/冗余；但作者指出这些优势的兑现依赖波形、频谱、接收处理和卫星资源，宽带 LEO 导航仍有基础与实践问题需要系统研究。

原文 · S1.p3.1 ↗
The authors in [ 6 ] analyzed the geometry of broadband LEO constellations and showed that their large number of satellites can substantially improve dilution of precision (DOP) performance compared with conventional navigation constellations.
原文 · S1.p4.1 ↗
Despite these promising developments, several fundamental and practical aspects of broadband LEO navigation still require systematic investigation.

作者报告

三条技术路线

作者把现有研究分为三条路线：机会式导航在接收端从现有通信下行提取同步、参考或导频结构；多源导航把 LEO 与 GNSS 或其他 PNT 源融合；ICAN 则从系统设计阶段把导航需求并入通信网络，联合信号、波束、卫星选择与资源调度。

对照原文证据
As illustrated in Fig. 1 , opportunistic navigation extracts navigation observables from existing communication downlinks at the receiver side, multisource navigation combines complementary measurements from different PNT sources, while ICAN incorporates navigation requirements directly into the design and operation of the communication network.
原文 · S2.SS3.p1.1 ↗
This route directly exploits existing LEO communication signals for PNT without requiring modifications to the satellite system.
原文 · S2.SS3.p2.1 ↗
LEO-based navigation can also be combined with other navigation sources rather than being used independently.
原文 · S2.SS3.p3.1 ↗
A more cooperative route is to incorporate PNT requirements into broadband LEO communication systems from the design stage.
原文 · S2.SS3.p4.1 ↗
作者报告

机会式与多源的分工

机会式路线不改卫星，依赖已知信号结构与卫星轨道/钟差信息，性能受信号知识可用性约束；多源路线利用 LEO 与 GNSS 在可用性、几何和测量类型上的互补，在 GNSS 受限环境中提供额外观测。

对照原文证据
Opportunistic navigation follows a similar receiver-side philosophy, with its performance depending strongly on signal knowledge and the availability of satellite orbit and clock information.
原文 · S2.SS3.p2.1 ↗
Such fusion is particularly attractive in GNSS-challenged environments, where LEO measurements can provide additional positioning information when GNSS visibility or signal quality is degraded.
原文 · S2.SS3.p3.1 ↗
作者报告

ICAN 的资源耦合

ICAN 把通信与导航放在同一资源池里联合设计，卫星选择、波束成形、功率与带宽都会产生通信—导航权衡；作者引用 LEO-ICAN 的联合卫星选择/波束优化，用速率与 GDOP 分别表征通信与导航性能。

对照原始英文摘要
The rapid deployment of broadband low Earth orbit (LEO) constellations provides new opportunities for next-generation navigation. Compared with conventional global navigation satellite systems (GNSS), broadband LEO systems provide stronger received signals, wider bandwidths, rapidly varying satellite geometry, and larger constellation scales, offering new capabilities for positioning, navigation, and timing (PNT). Meanwhile, their communication-oriented waveforms, high dynamics, and resource-constrained architectures introduce new challenges that are fundamentally different from those in GNSS. This article provides a comprehensive overview of broadband LEO constellations for next-generation navigation, covering their fundamental characteristics, representative use cases, key enabling technologies, and open challenges. We first compare broadband LEO systems with conventional GNSS and summarize the main technical routes for LEO-based PNT. We then discussed the emerging use cases in GNSS-challenged, multipath-dominant, and integrated communication and navigation (ICAN) scenarios. Key enabling technologies including waveform design, high-dynamic signal processing, and constellation resource scheduling are further reviewed. As a representative case study, the navigation-oriented optimization of the Starlink primary synchronization sequence demonstrates that subcarrier power allocation can improve multipath resistance while maintaining ranging accuracy. Finally, several challenges and future directions of broadband LEO-based navigation are discussed, aiming to provide reference for future researches.
