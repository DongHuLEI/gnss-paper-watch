arXiv 原文摘要

While current few-shot adaptation strategies for time series forecasting foundation models are predominantly dataset-centric, the necessity of obtaining an additional dataset for the finetuning process poses a significant hurdle for adoption. To address this constraint, we propose Sample-Wise Instance Fine-Tuning (SWIFT), a novel forecasting adaptation framework that achieves fine-grained specialization by leveraging structural priors from the target instance's own history. This shift allows the fine-tuning process to be entirely transparent to the end-user, which significantly enhances the ease of adoption. To implement this framework, SWIFT synergizes Self-Retrieval for historical mining and Frequency-Aware Loss for spectral consistency, while employing Deviation-Aware Dynamic Fusion to balance adaptation with general knowledge preservation. Extensive experiments across four TSFM backbones and three long-history benchmark subsets validate SWIFT, yielding average MASE and WQL reductions of 6.41% and 4.83% across 24 combinations of four backbones and six Chronos Benchmark II datasets. To the best of our knowledge, SWIFT is the first framework to investigate sample-wise instance fine-tuning of time-series foundation models, specializing the model for an individual prediction window using only the corresponding forecasting sample's own observed history.
