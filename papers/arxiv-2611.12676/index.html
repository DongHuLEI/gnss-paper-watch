整体概括

作者报告

指令给的是格名，不是坐标

这项视觉—语言—动作（VLA）实验让 SO-101 机械臂根据格名，在其余位置为空的棋盘上把国王移到指定格再归位。策略须从图像辨认格子，再完成夹取、搬运和释放。作者在同一任务上研究示教量、训练步数和模型选择；能下整盘棋只是另做的演示，没纳入这组定量实验。

对照原文证据
In this task, an SO-101 arm moves a chess king between two squares that are given only by their names, such as e4 and a5.
原文 · abstract1.1 ↗
The task is to pick up the king from one square of an otherwise empty chess board, place it on another square and return to the ready pose (Fig. 1 ). The source and target squares are given only in the instruction. We study the task on a real robot arm and in its simulated counterpart.
原文 · S2.p1.1 ↗
Beyond king moves, we also built a system on the same arm that plays full games with all pieces, but only as a demonstration, without evaluation or ablations.
原文 · S1.p3.1 ↗
作者报告

闭环成功率能改变训练决策

模拟器既能供给带变化的示教，也让各策略在同一套评测上比较。作者发现，示教数和更新量都影响闭环成功率；未经学习率冷却的检查点与冷却成品不能直接等价，验证损失和开环动作误差也可能给出错误排序。真机结果仅支持局部结论：模拟预训练在少量真机示教下有帮助。

对照原文证据
In a simulated version of the task, we present quantitative experiments indicating that the success rate scales with both data and updates, that checkpoints must be compared after a learning-rate cool-down or stochastic weight averaging (SWA) [ 5 ] , and that validation loss and action error are unreliable for model selection (Section 3.1 ).

On the real arm, partial results indicate that pretraining on simulated demonstrations helps when real data is scarce (Section 3.3 ).

02

Research gap

作者报告

训练损失更便宜，却不是最终指标

作者遇到的难题不是如何定义监督标签，而是已训练的策略该不该部署：实机每次测试需人复位，少量试次的差别又难解释。离线模仿误差只衡量记录输入上的动作偏差，不能直接计入策略动作改变后续观测所造成的失误。论文以单任务、大规模重复闭环试次回应，未做全领域查新。

对照原文证据
A central challenge is that closed-loop task success, the quantity of interest, is expensive to measure. On a real robot, each trial takes minutes and requires a person to reset the scene. It is therefore tempting to rely on offline proxies such as validation loss or action error, or on a few dozen trials per setting. Our study complements the fine-tuning experiments reported with the VLA models themselves [ 1 , 2 , 3 ] and data-scaling studies of imitation learning [ 4 ] with a detailed look at a single task, where a simulator lets us evaluate every model on many trials.
原文 · S1.p2.1 ↗
本站解释 · AI

仿真不是只提供更多相同轨迹

如果演示由总是沿同一路径的规划器产生，模仿策略就很少看到带误差的接近和补救。作者让脚本专家带着初始感知偏差、延迟与不同节奏行动，还可重试抓取；其轨迹与人类演示都呈散布，但真机人类搬运通常更直。路径差异提醒读者：模拟预训练给的是近似任务先验，不等于复刻了真机分布。

对照原文证据
A motion planner would execute every move identically, which neither resembles teleoperation nor gives enough variation for imitation learning. Our scripted expert therefore imitates the variability of a human operator. It reaches for a target with a perception error, observes the remaining error after a latency and corrects, and repeats this until the error is below a tolerance. Each episode samples its own tempo, errors, tolerances and grasp parameters, so paths and speeds differ between episodes (Fig. 2 ).
原文 · S2.SS2.p2.1 ↗
The paths of the human operator on the real arm show a similar spread, although the operator’s carries are straighter. The fine-tuned policies vary less than both the expert and the operator, in simulation and on the real arm.
原文 · S2.SS2.p3.1 ↗
The scripted expert is randomized but its similarity to human demonstrations is not rigorously measured.

先对齐两路视觉，再按指令定位

回放实验从基础权重另训，不是继续旧策略

作者以纯行为克隆策略采集带结果的回放，和原示教混合后，从基础权重另训模型，并与同更新量的纯示教基线比。除了只保留成功回放，还比较按整局成败贴标、按价值函数贴标、用不含当前回合的交叉拟合价值函数贴标。模拟教练则读取模拟器状态，接管后交还控制；这不是实机可直接观测的信号。

We compare four variants. Filtered BC trains on the demonstrations plus the successful rollouts. RECAP-style advantage conditioning [ 6 ] labels every frame with a learned value function. A cross-fitted variant of it labels each half of the episodes with a value function that never saw it. Outcome labels mark every frame of a success as positive and every frame of a failure as negative.
原文 · S3.SS2.p3.1 ↗
Variants. All variants train on the demonstrations plus the policy’s rollouts and are compared with behavior cloning on the demonstrations alone at the same number of updates.

优势标签按未来一秒的进展差定义

附录 E 用文字把一帧的优势定义成下一秒实际进展减去价值函数预期进展。阈值取全部训练帧（含示教）的优势分布第 30 百分位；仅超过阈值的回放帧记正，而示教帧无条件为正。成功回放帧有 72% 为正，失败回放中仍有 48% 为正，因此正标签不是整局成功的别名。

对照原文证据
A frame’s advantage is the progress made over the next second minus the progress the value function expects. The threshold is the 30th percentile of the advantages of all training frames, demonstrations included. Rollout frames above it are labeled positive, and every demonstration frame is labeled positive. This labels 72% of the frames of successful rollouts and 48% of those of failed rollouts positive.

模拟训练：成功子集嵌套，检查点成对比较

同一批约 11.4k 个脚本专家回合里，93% 成功。行为克隆的嵌套训练集只取成功示教，从 100 到 10k 回合，另留 30 个成功回合作验证。每组共用恒定学习率主干并从各目标预算的 90% 处分支，在最后 10% 把学习率降到零。每个配置只训练一次；分支比较省去了为每种预算各训一条主干。

对照原文证据
Data. The simulated demonstration set holds about 11.4k expert episodes with the piece offset by up to 8 mm inside its square, of which 93% are successes. Training sets are nested subsets of the successes with n=100 , 316, 1,000, 3.2k and 10k episodes, and 30 further successes are held out for validation.

For each dataset size, one constant-rate trunk keeps a checkpoint at 90% of every update budget, and a branch from each checkpoint cools the rate to zero over the remaining updates [ 10 , 11 ] . Each configuration is trained once.

模拟评测：动作结果与训练随机性分开

对照原始英文摘要
Vision-language-action (VLA) models are trained as general-purpose foundation models for robot learning, and require fine-tuning for each downstream task and robot. This raises practical questions such as how much data and training updates are needed, how checkpoints should be selected, and whether simulation can be leveraged to alleviate the cost of real data collection. We study these questions on a task that requires both precise manipulation and spatial understanding of the scene. In this task, an SO-101 arm moves a chess king between two squares that are given only by their names, such as e4 and a5. We fine-tune $π_{0.5}$ with various dataset sizes and update budgets, using teleoperated demonstrations as well as simulated demonstrations from a randomized scripted expert that imitates the variability of human demonstrations. In simulation, the success rate scales with both data and updates. Importantly, we find that a short learning-rate cool-down adds up to 31 points and is necessary for comparing checkpoints, and that validation loss and action error are unreliable for model selection. On the real arm, we fine-tune on up to 256 teleoperated demonstrations and evaluate ten policies in 25 trials each. Partial results indicate that pretraining on the simulated demonstrations helps substantially, most clearly when real data is scarce. We also present a negative result, in which training on the policy's own rollouts with filtered behavior cloning, RECAP-style advantage conditioning, corrections from an automatic coach, or residual reinforcement learning did not yield a measurable improvement over plain behavior cloning in our setting. We release the simulator, the expert and the real demonstrations.
