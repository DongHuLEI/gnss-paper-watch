先看结论与问题

摘要提出一种面向GNSS受限大范围环境的可扩展分布式声学监测系统：多个低成本MEMS麦克风阵列单元作为锚点，在边缘端用轻量SDES算法做声事件检测，再由TinyML分类器识别枪声；对检出的枪声用eGCC估计阵内TDoA，结合各单元AoA经改进SCO-EV框架融合得到全局坐标，并用MC算法补偿朝向误差。摘要报告在约4,985至49,063平方米范围内实验，枪声识别MCC为0.70，25,000平方米上平均定位误差低于6.5米，更大范围误差低于15米。

如何在缺乏GNSS、面积大且存在混响与低信噪比的野外场景中，用低成本分布式麦克风实时检测枪声并确定其全局位置（摘要以野生动物盗猎监测为背景）。

方法改变了什么

部署多个低成本MEMS麦克风阵列单元作为分布式锚点；每个单元边缘执行轻量SDES声事件检测与TinyML枪声分类；对检出的枪声用增强广义互相关eGCC估计阵内TDoA，据此得到AoA；各单元AoA经改进SCO-EV定位框架融合为全局坐标，并用misplacement calibration算法补偿平台朝向误差。

结果与证据

所提框架枪声识别MCC为0.70，在25,000平方米上平均定位误差低于6.5米，更大尺度下误差低于15米。

arXiv 原文摘要

This paper presents a scalable distributed acoustic surveillance system for real-time gunshot detection and localiza tion in large-area, GNSS-challenged environments. The system consists of multiple low-cost MEMS microphone array units (MAUs) deployed as distributed anchors. Each MAU performs edge-level sound-event detection using a lightweight Sound De tection and Echo Suppression (SDES) algorithm, followed by a TinyML-based classifier for gunshot identification. For detected gunshots, an enhanced Generalized Cross-Correlation (eGCC) al gorithm estimates intra-array time differences of arrival (TDoAs) with improved robustness under low signal-to-noise ratio and re verberant conditions. Angle-of-arrival (AoA) estimates obtained from individual MAUs are fused through a modified SCO-EV localization framework to determine the gunshot position in a global coordinate system. A misplacement calibration (MC) algorithm compensates for platform orientation errors, improv ing angular consistency and localization accuracy in practical field conditions. Extensive experiments conducted over coverage areas ranging from 4,985 m2 to 49,063 m2 demonstrate reliable operation across varying spatial scales. The proposed framework achieves a Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC) of 0.70 for gunshot recognition and average localization errors below 6.5 m over 25,000 m2 , while maintaining errors under 15 m at larger scales. The results demonstrate the practical potential of the proposed system for distributed gunshot surveillance in GNSS challenged large areas.
