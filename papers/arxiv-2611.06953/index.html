预测另一炉熟悉钢，还是预测从未见过的钢？

SteelAgent 以成分和热处理为输入，用冶金描述符和表格模型估计材料性能及区间，再由语言模型调用工具并组织解释。本文真正检验的不是对话好不好用，而是在换牌号、换钢族或换测量来源时，原有拉伸强度分数还剩多少参考价值。钢材数字取自 SteelBench 既有评估表的二次分析；另有合成实验，不是新一批钢材实测。

02

Research gap

作者报告

划分的单位应当对应部署承诺

同一牌号的不同炉次以及同一来源的测量，在随机切分时可能同时落入训练和测试折。这样的测试可以回答同类记录之间的插值问题，却不能直接证明对新牌号或新来源的外推。作者据此分别留出记录、牌号、钢族和来源；动机是把“未见”的含义从笼统标签变成工程上能复查的实验条件，而不是断言既有工作都存在数据泄漏。

对照原文证据
When an engineer requests a tensile-strength estimate for an alloy, the most relevant test set depends on what is already known about that alloy. Predicting another heat of a familiar grade, predicting an unfamiliar grade, and transferring a model to a new measurement source pose different questions. The distinction matters when the estimate guides alloy selection or prompts an engineer to order a qualification test.
原文 · S1.p1.1 ↗
Prior work has used machine learning to predict steel properties, design alloys and organize materials data ( Xiong et al., 2020 ; Wen et al., 2019 ; Choudhary et al., 2022 ) . Train–test overlap and choices about data partitioning can make performance difficult to interpret ( Kapoor and Narayanan, 2023 ) . In a random split, related grades and measurements from the same source may occur on both sides. An evaluation intended to establish transfer across grades or sources must explicitly withhold those units.
原文 · S1.p2.1 ↗
Four evaluation protocols probe different forms of transfer. Random k -fold assigns individual records to folds. Group k -fold withholds all records for a grade together. Leave-one-family-out (LOFO) withholds a metallurgical family. Leave-one-source-out (LOSO) withholds a provenance group. These protocols change group composition and sometimes sample size. They do not isolate a unique physical cause of the performance differences.
原文 · S3.p3.1 ↗
作者报告

在原来人群校准过，不代表换来源还能用

分割式共形预测的边际覆盖依赖校准与测试观测可交换。一个新来源可能有另一套残差分布，此时随机切分上的覆盖率不能视为它的下界。作者用最能暴露这一差距的对照：分别改变留出单位，并把区间宽度和实际覆盖同时列出，由读者检验更宽是否真的弥补了来源差异。

对照原文证据
The benchmark used conformalized quantile regression (CQR) with quantile-loss gradient boosting ( Romano et al., 2019 ) . Split conformal methods provide marginal coverage under exchangeability of calibration and test observations ( Vovk et al., 2005 ; Angelopoulos and Bates, 2023 ) . Holding out an entire source can change the residual distribution, so nominal coverage under exchangeability need not carry over unchanged ( Tibshirani et al., 2019 ) .
原文 · S6.p1.1 ↗
Mean widths varied from 357 to 412 MPa while realized coverage ranged from 37.6% to 91.0% (Table 3 ). Under source holdout the shortfall relative to nominal coverage was 52.4 percentage points. For a new source, a wide interval by itself offers little assurance that calibration remains appropriate.

池化分数高，不能反驳各族内分数低

全文还在行内定义折外池化决定系数和合规近似。本页不把行内式重新绘制成独立原式。池化评分用所有测试响应的总均值作参照，同一钢族内的分数却以该族均值和较局部的响应变差为分母。跨族差异可以抬高池化分数，即使各族内部排序和数值预测不够好；族内均值在这里是回顾性参照，不是部署时已知的预测。

对照原文证据
The headline outcome is ultimate tensile strength in MPa. We use the reported pooled out-of-fold coefficient of determination, R^{2}=1-\sum_{i}(y_{i}-\hat{y}_{i})^{2}/\sum_{i}(y_{i}-\bar{y})^{2} , and root mean squared error (RMSE). Each prediction in the pooled score comes from a fit that withheld its test fold. A score calculated within one held-out family uses that family’s own response mean and denominator. The pooled score can therefore be positive even when many within-family scores are negative.
原文 · S3.p2.1 ↗
A negative score means that the sum of squared errors exceeded the sum obtained by using the held-out group’s mean, a retrospective reference value.

合成数据另做岭回归，不是钢材 CQR 的重跑

作者用已知生成过程造出有组中心、组截距及非线性项的数据，分别做逐条随机留出、随机留两组和极端组留出，岭回归用分开的校准子集上绝对残差来定对称区间。最后一个情景同时改了响应函数，因此不能把它和第二个情景的差值只归给测试组更极端。已通读官方附带脚本；脚本中的随机设置是单次随机分训练和测试再拆校准集，不是对钢材表进行交叉验证。

对照原文证据
We also tested coverage in a regression problem with a known data-generating process. Each of 1,636 observations belongs to one of twelve groups. The eleven-dimensional covariate vector is the sum of a group center drawn from N(0,4I) and independent N(0,0.64I) noise. The response combines a linear term, an interaction, a squared feature, a random group intercept and independent Gaussian noise. We fit ridge regression with penalty one and calibrate symmetric intervals on absolute residuals from a separate calibration set.

In the second, we held out two randomly selected groups. The last setting changes the response function as well as the choice of test groups, so the difference from the second setting cannot be attributed to group selection alone.

对照原始英文摘要
Steel property models can perform well on random test splits even when they extrapolate poorly to unfamiliar alloys. SteelAgent couples metallurgical descriptors and machine-learning models with a language-model interface for property prediction, uncertainty reporting and inverse design. We examine how the choice of held-out unit changes the conclusions drawn from SteelBench tensile-strength evaluations. For gradient boosting, pooled out-of-fold $R^2$ fell from 0.859 on a random split to 0.113 when steel families were held out. Empirical coverage of nominal 90% prediction intervals ranged from 91.0% under random splitting to 37.6% on held-out sources. The effect of physics-derived features depended on the evaluation. They increased gradient-boosting $R^2$ by 0.2534 in a 17-family holdout analysis and decreased it by 0.358 in a separate four-grade laboratory analysis. We report group-level errors and a synthetic regression experiment illustrating how calibration can deteriorate on held-out groups. These results motivate reporting the evaluation population alongside a property estimate or compliance score.
