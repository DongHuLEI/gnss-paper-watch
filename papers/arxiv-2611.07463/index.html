系统与核心主张

FREE-LIVO 是面向模块化/异构机器人的紧耦合 LIVO：不要求 LiDAR–相机刚性支架或时间同步；用预训练图像匹配器把相机图像与单帧 LiDAR 经生成模型上采样得到的稠密强度图匹配，由三维点—像素对应构造重投影因子，再与点云配准、视觉跟踪及各传感器 IMU 约束放进同一因子图。

this paper presents FREE-LIVO, a tightly coupled odometry method that requires neither a rigid LiDAR–camera rig nor LiDAR–camera time synchronization.

The proposed method uses a pretrained image feature matcher to establish correspondences between a camera image and a dense LiDAR intensity image generated from a single LiDAR scan using a generative model.
原文 · abstract1.1 ↗
the proposed system is based on a tight coupling of LiDAR, camera, and IMU constraints, meaning that the raw observations from these sensors are fused in a single factor graph.

物理解耦与紧耦合的实际含义

物理解耦指 LiDAR 与相机不要求刚性固定、外参或时间同步；紧耦合指原始 LiDAR、视觉、IMU 约束与跨模态重投影因子在同一因子图联合优化，而不是各自算里程计再融合位姿。它仍使用相机内参和每路 IMU 观测，也没有声称完全不需要任何标定。

Research gap

作者报告

现有 LIVO 的刚性与同步假设

作者把缺口放在现有 LIVO 的共同假设：LiDAR、相机、IMU 刚性安装、外参已知且时间同步；外参或时间误差会让 LiDAR 点与图像像素错位，削弱跨模态约束。R3LIVE 虽估计相对位姿与时间偏移，但假设二者保持不变。

对照原文证据
most existing LIVO systems assume that the LiDAR, camera, and IMU are rigidly mounted on a common platform with known extrinsic parameters and accurate time synchronization.

Even small errors in these parameters can misalign LiDAR measurements and image pixels, weakening the cross-modal constraints and degrading state estimation accuracy
原文 · S2.SS2.p2.1 ↗
R3LIVE [ 15 ] estimates the relative pose and time offset between the LiDAR and camera but assumes that both remain constant.

模块化与异构机器人的需求

模块化/异构系统中，传感器可以装在独立运动的执行器、移动传感单元甚至不同机器人上；LiDAR–相机相对位姿随时间变化，严格同步也可能不可得，传统 LIVO 的假设失效。

本文的回应与边界

已有跨模态 LiDAR–视觉匹配多用于全局定位/地点识别，比较全局特征来检索地点，不做连续的点/像素级位姿估计；本文把细粒度对应放进因子图，用重投影因子连续连接两条传感器轨迹。它证明的是这类对应可用于里程计，不是所有跨模态匹配器都能直接替换。

因子图结构：三条约束链

因子图分三部分：标准 LiDAR–IMU 里程计（点云配准加 IMU 预积分）、视觉–IMU 里程计（smart visual factors 加 IMU）、以及连接两者的 LiDAR–相机重投影因子；两条状态序列独立创建，跨模态因子可连接不同时间戳的位姿，因此不需要固定相对外参或常数时间偏移状态。

对照原始英文摘要
LiDAR-Inertial-Visual Odometry (LIVO) integrates complementary LiDAR, camera, and IMU measurements to achieve robust state estimation. However, most existing methods assume a fixed relative pose and accurate time synchronization between sensors. Toward modular and heterogeneous robotic systems, this paper presents FREE-LIVO, a tightly coupled odometry method that requires neither a rigid LiDAR-camera rig nor LiDAR-camera time synchronization. The proposed method uses a pretrained image feature matcher to establish correspondences between a camera image and a dense LiDAR intensity image generated from a single LiDAR scan using a generative model. From the resulting 3D point-to-pixel correspondences, we construct reprojection factors that directly constrain LiDAR and camera poses at different acquisition times. These factors are jointly optimized in a single factor graph together with constraints from point cloud registration, visual feature tracking, and the IMU measurements associated with each sensor. On six sequences from the public M3DGR dataset, FREE-LIVO achieves an average absolute trajectory error (ATE) RMSE of 0.45 m without provided LiDAR-camera extrinsic parameters and exhibits minimal accuracy degradation under time synchronization errors of up to 0.5 s. Furthermore, experiments with the LiDAR and camera mounted on separate robots demonstrate that the method can jointly estimate the trajectories of independently moving sensors.
