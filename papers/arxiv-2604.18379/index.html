预报的是视线事件概率，不是连续电离层图

IonoDGNN 把每条活动的卫星—接收机视线（LoS）映射到电离层薄壳穿刺点（IPP）。在新加坡共址接收机的多星座数据上，任务以 5 分钟为节拍、最长提前 2 小时，输出每个未来节点是否处于经质控的 TEC 变化率指数（ROTI）事件中的概率。输出不是 ROTI 幅值，也不是定位误差。

固定网格和固定站点图没有记住观测怎样移动

作者把缺口放在采样结构而不是插值精度。格网产品适合表达大尺度场，却把稀疏的沿视线观测变成受假设影响的网格估计；等离子体泡等局地锐变可能被平滑。固定网格节点或固定接收机节点的图也没有跟着卫星升落而重构。这里转向保留原生视线，但这不是已做过等价格网基线的全面优越性检验。

星历提供未来观测几何这个已知条件：卫星进入仰角掩膜后，视线虽然没有自身历史，仍然有位置、仰角和几何邻居可用于推断。这个赌注可以被证伪：如果未来几何或邻居信息没用，移除条件化或在无历史子集只做概率插值时，不应损失技巧。未来真实观测从未作为预测输入。

算法主线

作者报告

按时刻成图，再按身份对齐

同一时刻以穿刺点薄壳距离连接近邻；边含距离、地理与地磁坐标差、地方太阳时差及共址同星标志。每条视线用站点、星座和卫星身份对齐历史与未来，身份不进模型。历史存在、未来存在、标签合法和无历史是不同掩码，不能把填充位置当安静样本。

At each snapshot, the edges carry the features \mathbf{X}_{E}^{t} : geodetic distance, differences in geographic and geomagnetic coordinates, angular separation in local solar time, and a binary flag indicating whether two nodes share the same satellite across the zero-baseline pair.

Node identities (station, constellation, satellite) are known and consistent across time.
原文 · S3.SS2.p1.1 ↗
Node identities serve only for alignment, padding, and mask construction; they are never provided as input features. This batching procedure is tailored to our setting where per-timestep graphs are small (mean \approx 26 nodes), so padding overhead is negligible.
原文 · S3.SS2.p3.1 ↗
Four binary masks of shape \{0,1\}^{B\times 24\times N_{\max}} are defined over the padded unified node axis to control what the model sees, how it learns, and what it predicts on. \mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{hist}} is indexed over the history timesteps, while \mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{pred}} , \mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{valid}} , and \mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{nohist}} are indexed over the forecast timesteps.

作者报告

预测窗只走星历，不回灌未来真值

历史模块先在各时刻经节点编码和边偏置图注意力，再沿每条视线做掩码时序注意力，得历史嵌入。预测模块将星历编码与历史嵌入由融合网络合并，再跨未来图作空间、时间聚合，一次输出各时刻概率。无历史节点的自身历史嵌入退化成无观测信息的学习常量，但未来图可把有历史邻居的信息传给它。

对照原文证据
A spatial GNN, implemented as multi-head graph attention [ 40 ] with the edge features \mathbf{X}_{E}^{\tau} biasing the attention scores, then propagates information across that step’s graph. Temporal self-attention [ 41 ] with sinusoidal positional encoding, masked by \mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{hist}} , links the per-step embeddings of each node. The resulting per-node summary \mathbf{h}_{\mathrm{hist}} is then passed to the prediction module.

At each forecast step, a node encoder embeds these features, a fusion MLP combines the embedding with \mathbf{h}_{\mathrm{hist}} , and a spatial GNN propagates information across the predicted graph. A second temporal attention, masked by \mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{pred}} , couples the embeddings across the forecast horizon, and a classification head yields the per-node, per-step disturbance probabilities \mathbf{P}^{\,t+1:t+24} .
原文 · S3.I2.i2.p1.1 ↗
Nodes flagged by \mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{nohist}} are excluded from history encoding by the history mask \mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{hist}} , so their \mathbf{h}_{\mathrm{hist}} reduces to a learned constant that carries no observational information. Their context is instead recovered through spatial message passing from neighboring IPPs that carry historical state.

先算概率误差，再相对气候参考归一化

Brier 分数把所有计分节点—提前量对的概率平方误差一起聚合。以原文给定的事件气候率计算参照后，Brier 技巧分数才说明相对气候参考的收益。它不等于相对持续性提升；分提前量的曲线与汇总表也不是同一个统计量。概率技巧不能单独证明校准，因此仍值得单看可靠性曲线。

05

实验怎么做

作者报告

用双站片段核验事件

新加坡 NTUS 与 SIN1 观测先由双频载波相位算出 ROTI，再降到建模时间率。标签不是历元幅值阈值：片段峰值低于 0.3 TECU/min 归为安静；同卫星双站候选片段重叠需覆盖较短片段至少 50% 才确认，确认后整段继承事件标签。不确定和仅一站异常的片段与安静分开。

对照原文证据
Per-satellite ROTI is derived from dual-frequency geometry-free carrier phase following the original definition of Pi et al. [ 1 ] . The geometry-free combination isolates the ionospheric delay. From this delay, relative slant total electron content (TEC), rate of TEC (ROT), and its 5-minute standard deviation, the ROT index (ROTI), are computed with a 30-second cadence.
原文 · S2.SS2.p1.1 ↗
Segments whose peak ROTI falls below 0.3 TECU/min are reclassified as quiet. This value separates the bell-shaped quiet-time mode from the long-tailed event distribution on a log-scaled amplitude histogram. For segments whose peak exceeds this threshold, the peak is traced forward and backward to the nearest baseline condition, and all timesteps within this interval are labeled as event.
原文 · S2.SS3.p2.1 ↗
Confirmed (+1): Both stations detect an event on the same satellite, and the temporal intersection of the two segments covers at least 50% of the shorter segment.
原文 · S2.I1.i1.p1.1 ↗
Confirmation is a segment-level decision: once a segment is corroborated, every timestep within it inherits the event label, without requiring second-station coverage at each epoch.
原文 · S2.SS3.p4.1 ↗

对照原始英文摘要
Most data-driven ionospheric models operate on gridded products, which do not preserve the time-varying sampling structure of satellite-based sensing. We instead model the ionosphere as a dynamic graph over ionospheric pierce points, with connectivity that evolves as satellite positions change. Because satellite trajectories are predictable, the graph topology over the forecast horizon can be constructed in advance. We exploit this property to condition forecasts on the future graph structure, which we term ephemeris conditioning. This enables prediction on lines of sight (LoS) that appear only in the forecast horizon. We evaluate our framework on Global Navigation Satellite System data from a co-located receiver pair in Singapore spanning 2023 to 2025. The task is forecasting irregularities defined by the Rate of TEC Index (ROTI) up to 2 hours ahead as per-node binary classification. The resulting model, IonoDGNN, achieves a Brier Skill Score (BSS) of 0.55 and an area under the precision-recall curve (PR-AUC) of 0.77. These correspond to improvements over persistence of 53% in BSS and 58% in PR-AUC, with larger gains at longer lead times.
